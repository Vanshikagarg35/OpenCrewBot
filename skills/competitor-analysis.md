# Competitor Analysis

For each competitor find:
- Name and website
- Main products / services
- Public pricing (write "not public" if not found, dont make up price)
- Target customers
- Positioning (how they describe themself)
- Strengths and weaknesses

Then make a comparison table in markdown:

| Competitor | Products | Pricing | Target | Positioning |
|---|---|---|---|---|

End with 3-5 gaps or opportunities for us. Every claim should have a link.
