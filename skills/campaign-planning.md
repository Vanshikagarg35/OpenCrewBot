# Campaign Planning

1. Audience - who exactly, what they worry about, where they spend time online
2. Key message + 2-3 campaign concepts (name, idea, why it works)
3. Channels (Instagram, YouTube shorts, WhatsApp, school events etc)
4. 30 day content calendar as table: Day | Channel | Format | Topic | CTA
5. Sample campaign assets - write these out fully, dont just describe them:
   - 2 Instagram captions (with hashtags)
   - 1 reel / short video script (hook, 3 scenes, CTA)
   - 1 WhatsApp message for parents (short)
   - 1 email subject + first 3 lines

Don't promise results that we can't prove (like "100% admission").
