# Email Drafting

- Subject line under 60 characters
- First line should say why you are writing
- Short paragraphs, one clear call to action
- Match the tone from user preferences (check USER.md)
- NEVER send directly. Sending is an external action and needs approval.

Output: Subject, Body, and who it is meant for.
