# Market Research

Steps:
1. Define what market we are looking at (country, segment, customer type).
2. Search for recent sources (last 12-18 months is better).
3. Find market size numbers only if a source gives them. Do not guess numbers.
4. List main customer segments and what they need.
5. Write down trends and opportunities.

Output format:
- Summary (3-4 lines)
- Key findings with source link for each
- Opportunities
- What could not be verified
